Our Story & Ethos

We believe that architecture is the quiet backdrop to extraordinary lives.

North & Key was founded on a singular conviction: that acquiring or selling a home of genuine architectural merit should be treated as a cultural stewardship, not merely a commercial exchange.

Malibu Coastline Atelier • California
Guiding Values

The Three Standards

01

Curation Over Volume

We actively limit our portfolio to residences that possess structural honesty, proportional beauty, and material integrity. We choose to represent fewer properties with profound dedication.

02

The Monograph Approach

Rather than generic MLS snippets, we produce architectural monographs, cinematic documentaries, and scholarly provenance reports that elevate the property's cultural value in global circles.

03

Radical Discretion

A significant portion of our transactions never appear in the public sphere. We safeguard client privacy with uncompromising institutional protocol across our global advisory desks.
